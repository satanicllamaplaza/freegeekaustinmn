Lets Close the Circuit

Refurbish, Reuse, Revitalize Right Here

Every day valuble hardware leaves our communities. It may get recycled but it never comes back. We are forced to pay obsorbanant costs to bring more in.

What if we stopped shipping it out?

Every computer that we let leave is a loss for our community. When you donate to us you give that computer another chance to help your neighbor. We are all watching the cost of electronics sky rocket. It's a financial drain on a community that already owns the equipment.

We take donations in and we donate them right back out!

Once we fix up your donated equipment we place it with someone who needs it. A refurbished computer can help someone:

  • Do Their homework
  • Find a Job
  • Learn New Skills & Passions
  • Develop Financial Stability
  • Or Just Relax

We have what we need. We just need to keep it!
